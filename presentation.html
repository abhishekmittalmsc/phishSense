Solution

What does PhishSense do?

🔍
Scans automatically
Reads the email as soon as you open it — no button to click.
🧠
Gives a risk score
AI rates the email from 0–100 and tells you if it's safe, suspicious, or phishing.
🎯
Spots manipulation
Detects tactics like urgency, fear, and authority with quotes from the email.
🏢
Catches BEC attacks
Flags when the sender's display name doesn't match their email address.
🔗
Checks every link
Scans all URLs in the email against VirusTotal to catch malicious sites.
📋
Clear report
Shows a plain-English summary with a full breakdown in the side panel.
